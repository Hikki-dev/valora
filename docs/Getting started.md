# Getting started

This page walks through Valora one screen at a time, in the order you will meet them. It takes about five minutes to read and less to do.

You can use Valora on the website or on your phone. Everything on this page works the same on both.

## Before you start

You need an email address, or a Google account. That is all. There is nothing to install to use the website.

If you want to run your own copy instead, see [Running it yourself](Running%20it%20yourself.md).

## Step 1: Sign in

Open the website and choose how to sign in. **Continue with Google** is one tap. Or type an email and a password, and use **New here? Create an account** the first time.

On a computer the page shows a picture beside the form. On a phone the Valora logo sits at the top and the form fills the screen.

Valora never sees your Google password. Read more in [Your privacy and data](Your%20privacy%20and%20data.md).

## Step 2: Add your first game

Press the add button and pick one of three ways:

| Way | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Search** | Any game. Type the name and pick it from the list |
| **Scan** | A boxed game. Point your phone at the barcode |
| **Sync Steam** | Your whole Steam library in one go |

For a game you own on disc, choose how it is kept: **Game only** (just the disc or cartridge), **Complete** (game, case and manual) or **Sealed** (still in the shrink-wrap). The condition matters, because a sealed game sells for much more than a loose disc.

If you paid for the game, add what you paid. Valora uses it to show whether you are up or down.

## Step 3: Read your Home page

Home answers one question: what is my collection worth today?

* The big number is the total value of everything you own.
* The chart shows how it moved over time.
* Your most valuable games are listed under it.

When Valora has no price for a game, it shows a dash. It never fills in a number it made up, and a dash is left out of your total.

## Step 4: Browse your Library

Library lists every game you own.

* **Search** waits for you to stop typing, so it stays smooth even with thousands of games.
* **Sort** by value (the default), by name, or by most recently added.
* Switch between a **grid** of covers and a **list**.

Open a game to see its page.

## Step 5: A game's page

Each game has its own page with:

* its current value, and what you paid
* a price range chart that fills in as days go by
* a card for each condition, so you can see what a loose copy and a boxed copy are worth
* buttons to refresh the prices, edit the game, or delete it

If you delete a game by mistake, you get a few seconds to press **Undo**.

## Step 6: Keep a Wishlist

Add games you want, and set a **target price**. When the market price drops to your target, the game is marked as hit, so you know it is time to buy.

## Step 7: See where your money is

Insights gives you three numbers: how many of your games have a price, the average value, and your top game. Under them, your games are ranked from most to least valuable. It is a good page to look at before you decide what to sell.

Games with no market price yet are not ranked. Insights tells you how many there are.

## Step 8: Share a snapshot

Press **Share** to make a clean picture of your ten most valuable games. Post it, or keep it for yourself.

## Step 9: Make it yours

In Settings you can:

* switch between **light** and **dark** mode. Valora remembers your choice
* show prices in **US dollars** or **Sri Lankan rupees**
* **hide values**, so you can show someone your shelf without showing the numbers
* read a short explanation of **where prices come from**
